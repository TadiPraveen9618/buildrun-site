Last updated: 25 November 2026

BuildRun Digital ("we", "us") is a Hyderabad-based service that builds landing pages and manages Meta and Google Ads. This policy explains what we collect on this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you submit in our website form or in a lead form on Facebook or Instagram: name, WhatsApp/phone number, business type, service needed and budget range.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook.
  • Technical and usage data through cookies and similar tools (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Google Ads), such as pages viewed, device, approximate location and the ad that brought you here.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry on WhatsApp or phone.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising, including sending conversion data to Meta and Google. Contact details sent for this are hashed (encrypted one-way) before sharing.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers named above (Google, Meta, and Google Sheets/Gmail where we store enquiries), to run and measure this website and our ads. We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for up to 24 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time by messaging us on the contact details below. We will respond within 30 days, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can also block cookies in your browser, opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout, and manage ad preferences in your Google and Meta account settings.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no method of storage or transfer online is fully secure.

Children

Our services are meant for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its date.
